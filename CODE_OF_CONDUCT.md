# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Everyone should be able to participate in bookmark-xport regardless of age,
disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, experience, nationality,
appearance, race, religion, or sexual orientation.

Be respectful, welcome questions, give constructive feedback, respect privacy,
and take accessibility needs seriously. Critique ideas and code rather than
people. Accept corrections and make room for different experiences.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexual attention, personal
attacks, deliberate disruption, and publishing someone else's private
information without permission are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces.
Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, request a correction, limit
participation, or remove a participant. Responses should reflect the impact
and repetition of the behavior. Maintainers are also bound by this policy.

## Reporting

Use GitHub's **Report content** action on abusive content, or
[report abuse to GitHub](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse),
especially if a maintainer is involved. Do not post sensitive incident details
or personal information in a public issue.

For a non-sensitive moderation concern, contact the maintainer through a
[project issue](https://github.com/mickpletcher/bookmark-xport/issues/new).
You may request a private contact channel there without describing the incident.
This repository does not currently publish a separate private conduct-reporting
address. GitHub's abuse-reporting route remains available without a public issue.

Security vulnerabilities use [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not this process.
